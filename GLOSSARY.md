# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to students and teams. This glossary covers who uses it, course access, team licences, and refunds.

## Language

### People

**Student**:
Someone who buys courses or claims Seats and watches lessons.
_Avoid_: Learner, User

**Instructor**:
Speaks for their own courses only. An Instructor builds and edits the courses they own, and sees the students enrolled on them.
_Avoid_: Teacher, Author, Creator

**Admin**:
A member of the Cadence team who runs the platform and decides Refund Requests. Not the same as a team Admin, who only buys and shares a team's Seats.
_Avoid_: Staff, Reviewer, Moderator

### Courses

**Course Access**:
A student's right to watch a course's lessons. It is granted by buying the course or claiming a Seat, and taken away when a Refund Request is approved.
_Avoid_: Enrollment (for the right itself), Entitlement, Licence

**Progress**:
What a student has done in a course: lessons completed, time watched, and quiz attempts. It belongs to the student and survives losing Course Access.
_Avoid_: Completion (for the whole record)

**Watched**:
Seconds of video a Student has actually played in a course: the furthest point reached in each lesson, added up. Not lessons opened.
_Avoid_: Viewed, Progress

**Lapsed**:
A Student on an Instructor's roster who has Progress in a course but no longer has Course Access, usually after an Approved Refund Request.

### Teams

**Team Licence**:
A purchase of several Seats in one course, made by a team Admin for their team. Buying one doesn't give the buyer Course Access themselves.
_Avoid_: Team purchase, Bulk purchase

**Seat**:
One person's Course Access that comes from a Team Licence. A Seat is either unclaimed or claimed by one student.

**Seat Code**:
The link a team Admin shares so that someone can claim a Seat. It stops working once its Seat is claimed, while the Team Licence has an Open Refund Request, and for good once that request is approved.
_Avoid_: Coupon

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A student's ask, with their reason, to get back what they paid for a purchase. The team approves or declines it. One purchase can have several Refund Requests over time, but only one can be undecided at once.
_Avoid_: Refund (for the ask itself), Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Open Refund Request**:
A Refund Request the team hasn't decided yet.
_Avoid_: Pending, Requested, Awaiting review

**Approved Refund Request**:
A Refund Request an Admin has accepted. Approving it ends the student's Course Access, or for a Team Licence cancels all its Seat Codes, and leads to a Refund.

**Declined Refund Request**:
A Refund Request an Admin has turned down, with a note to the student explaining why. It has no Refund, and the student may raise a new Refund Request.

**Watch Limit**:
A Refund is only offered on a course with under one hour Watched. Past that, the Student has had the course, and the refund form turns them away. It doesn't apply to Team Licences.
_Avoid_: Watch cap, Viewing threshold

**Refund**:
The money returned to a student after their Refund Request is approved. It is always the full amount they paid. A declined Refund Request has no Refund.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation

**Pending Refund**:
A Refund the team has approved where the money hasn't been confirmed as sent yet.
_Avoid_: Pending (for a Refund Request awaiting review)

**Issued Refund**:
A Refund where the money has been confirmed as sent back to the student.
_Avoid_: Completed, Paid out

**Failed Refund**:
A Refund where the money couldn't be sent. The Refund Request stays approved, because the decision hasn't changed.
